# Privacy policy

Princeps does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

The extension runs only on linkedin.com. Its only action is to uncheck the Easy Apply checkbox whose label reads “Follow [company] to stay up to date with their page” when that box is selected. It does not read the page for any other purpose, does not fill in or submit applications, and does not change any other control.

Nothing you do on LinkedIn is sent to the developer or to any other server. Princeps has no account, no analytics, and no remote code.

Princeps is unofficial and is not affiliated with LinkedIn.
